<h2>Advisor</h2>

<div class="profile-container">
  <img src="/images/logo.png" alt="Prof. Jinghui Zhong" class="profile-img">
  <div class="profile-content">
    <strong><a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=AO5BsHwAAAAJ" target="_blank">Prof. Jinghui Zhong (钟竞辉)</a></strong> is a Full Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, South China University of Technology (SCUT). He was selected as a Young Top-notch Scholar under Guangdong's High-Level Talent Program and has been consistently ranked among Stanford's World's Top 2% Scientists.
    <br><br>
    He received his B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in Computer Science from Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU) in 2005, 2007, and 2012, respectively. From 2013 to 2016, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow with the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. In 2016, he joined SCUT where he currently leads research in the Computational Intelligence Team.
    <br><br>
    His research interests include <b>Intelligent Optimization & Decision-Making</b>, <b>Evolutionary Computation & Genetic Programming</b>, <b>Explainable AI for Science (Symbolic Regression)</b>, and <b>Multimodal Foundation Models</b>. He has published over 100 papers in premier international journals and conferences, including more than 40 in IEEE/ACM Transactions (TEVC, TCYB, TITS, TAI, CSUR, etc.). Dr. Zhong has served as Vice Chair of the IEEE CIS Guangzhou Chapter, Senior Member of IEEE and CCF, and Editorial Board Member of <i>Memetic Computing</i> and <i>ICT Express</i>. He has been awarded the IEEE TETCI Outstanding Paper Award (2023) and the First-Class Natural Science Award by the Ministry of Education (2010).
    <br><br>

<h2>Researchers & Graduate Students</h2>

<div class="profile-container">
  <div class="profile-content">
    <p>Our team brings together postdoctoral fellows, Ph.D. students, master's students, and undergraduate researchers working at the cutting edge of AI, evolutionary computation, and scientific discovery.</p>
    
    <h3>Current Research Cohorts</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><b>Postdoctoral Researchers & Ph.D. Candidates:</b> Focusing on symbolic regression foundations, LLM-augmented heuristic optimization, transfer and multitask evolutionary computation, and complex dynamic control.</li>
      <li><b>Master's Students:</b> Developing benchmark toolkits, reinforcement learning algorithms for network lifetime maximization, and intelligent transportation modeling.</li>
      <li><b>Undergraduate Scholars:</b> Engaged in foundational algorithm implementation, equation discovery benchmarks, and open-source software maintenance.</li>
    </ul>
