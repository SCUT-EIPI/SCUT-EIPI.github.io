    <p>
      <b>Prof. Jinghui Zhong</b> is a Full Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, South China University of Technology (SCUT). He leads an interdisciplinary AI research and engineering matrix of 85+ scholars, directing end-to-end innovation across foundational algorithms, autonomous agent frameworks, edge computing deployment, and real-world industrial validations.
    </p>
    <p>
      He received his B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in Computer Science from Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU) in 2005, 2007, and 2012, respectively. From 2013 to 2016, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow with the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. In 2016, he joined SCUT where he leads research in the Computational Intelligence Team.
    </p>
    <p>
      His research interests center on <b>Intelligent Optimization & Decision-Making</b>, <b>Evolutionary Computation & Genetic Programming</b>, <b>Explainable AI for Science (Symbolic Regression)</b>, and <b>Multimodal Foundation Models</b>. He has published over 150 papers in premier international journals and conferences, including more than 40 in IEEE/ACM Transactions (TEVC, TCYB, TITS, TAI, CSUR, etc.). Dr. Zhong has served as Vice Chair of the IEEE CIS Guangzhou Chapter, Senior Member of IEEE and CCF, and Editorial Board Member of <i>Memetic Computing</i> and <i>ICT Express</i>. He has been awarded the First-Class Natural Science Award by the Ministry of Education (2010) and the IEEE TETCI Outstanding Paper Award (2023).
    </p>
