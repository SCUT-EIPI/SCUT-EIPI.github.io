    <span class="award-sub">IEEE Transactions on Emerging Topics in Computational Intelligence Outstanding Paper Award</span>
  </div>
  <div class="award-body">
    <p><b>获奖论文：</b> <i>An Adaptive Archive-Based Evolutionary Framework for Many-Task Optimization</i></p>
    <p><b>作者团队：</b> Liang Feng, Jinghui Zhong*, and Kay Chen Tan</p>
    <p><b>成果亮点：</b> 针对复杂多任务优化场景中任务间负迁移严重的难题，团队首创了基于任务分布自适应建模与在线表征学习的多任务演化框架。该框架有效抑制了负迁移并显著提升搜索效率，成为演化多任务计算领域的奠基性工作之一。</p>
    <div class="award-meta">
